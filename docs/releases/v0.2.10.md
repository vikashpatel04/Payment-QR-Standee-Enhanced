Info Island settings. Installed copies update to it automatically.

## New

- **The Info Island has its own section in Settings**, with a live preview of the island at its real size (point at it to see the ×):
  - **Size**, from 60% to 140%, to suit each monitor. The island is now a little smaller by default (85%).
  - **Corner roundness** of its lower corners.
  - **Animation speed**: fast, medium or slow.
  - **Show on screen** puts a sample island at the top of your screen for a few seconds, so you can judge the size on the monitor itself.
- Changes apply straight away, even to an island that is showing.
- The island's text uses the same font as the amount pill.
