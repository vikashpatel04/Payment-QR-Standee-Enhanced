A layout update for E-ink displays. Installed copies update to it automatically.

## Changes

- **The QR has one fixed place.** On every E-ink screen that shows a QR (Pay, Your UPI QR, the rating slide and Thanks) it sits in the same square: at the top of an upright panel, on the left of a wide one. The words always start below it, under a divider at the same height on every screen.
- **Shop names wrap between words, never inside one.** A two-word name can take two lines ("Sample / Textiles"), but a word is never split across lines. The font shrinks until every word fits, and a single word too long for the line is narrowed instead of broken.
