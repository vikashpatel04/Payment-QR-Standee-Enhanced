## Fixes

- **Focus really goes back to your billing window after Enter.** After handing focus back, the popup also asked Windows to let go of it, and Windows passed focus on to the next window behind the popup (often the taskbar) instead. It no longer does that, so you can keep typing in your billing software straight away.
- **A quicker "Sent".** Once the display has shown the QR, the pill turns green, shows the tick and slides out in under half a second.
