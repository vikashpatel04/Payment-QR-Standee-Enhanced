## New

- **Info Island** (Settings > General, off unless you turn it on). After Enter, the amount pill drops away at once and a small black island slides down from the top edge of the screen. It shows the amount and a ring counting down the time the QR has left:
  - The dot pulses until a display has drawn the QR, then glows green.
  - It turns red with "Display not connected" when the QR is only on the simulator.
  - The ring turns amber for the last 10 seconds.
  - Point at the island and a × slides in. Clicking it only hides the island; the QR stays on the customer display.
  - Shift+F9 shows "QR cleared" on the island instead of the usual message at the bottom. A QR that runs out of time shows "QR timed out". Then the island tucks back into the top edge.

  The island never takes focus from your billing software, lets clicks through everywhere except on itself, and follows Reduce animations. With it off, everything works exactly as before.
