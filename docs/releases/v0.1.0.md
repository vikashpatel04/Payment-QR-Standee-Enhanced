The first build of Counter Pay: show the customer an exact-amount UPI QR with one shortcut, a few digits and Enter.

## Install

1. Download **counter-pay-setup-0.1.0.exe** below and run it. It installs for your Windows user only (no admin prompt) and starts at login.
2. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose **More info → Run anyway**.
3. First-run setup: import your merchant standee QR (photo, screenshot or pasted text), check the shortcuts, pick a display, and do the ₹1 amount-lock test.

From now on Counter Pay updates itself from this page. Updates download in the background and install only when you quit or in quiet hours (10 pm–8 am, no QR showing). Change that in **Settings → Updates**.

## In this version

- **F9** opens the amount pill, **Enter** shows the QR, **Shift+F9** clears it. Focus goes straight back to your billing window. (F12 can't be used: Windows reserves it.)
- Customer displays: this PC (simulator), a second monitor, or a phone or tablet on the shop Wi-Fi with a pairing code.
- Pay, Thanks and Ambient scenes in English plus one Indian language: Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi or Odia.
- Every QR ends on Clear, a new amount, its time limit (3 minutes) or a lost display link.
- History of every QR shown, with daily totals and CSV export for Excel.
- Content: shop name, logo, accent colour, rating QR and text slides.
