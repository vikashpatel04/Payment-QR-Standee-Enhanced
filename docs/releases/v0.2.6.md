A small update. Installed copies update to it automatically.

## New

- **A clear "Sent" in the popup.** When the display shows the QR, the ₹ turns into a tick, the pill fills with your accent colour and says **Sent** in big letters, with the amount underneath. Then it fades away as before, with no extra wait.
- **Reduce animations** (Settings > General). Turn it on for simple fades instead of moving and growing effects in the popup, the toasts and the Counter Pay window. Useful on slow PCs. The popup and toasts already keep it simple when Windows' "Show animations" setting is off.

## Fixes

- **Focus goes back to your billing window after Enter, even with an E-ink display.** The popup still waits until the display shows the QR (an E-ink panel takes a few seconds), but if Counter Pay held on to focus during that wait, it now hands it back when the popup closes. A click into another program in the meantime is left alone.
