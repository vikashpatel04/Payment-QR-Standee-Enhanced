More control over the standby slides. Installed copies update to it automatically.

## New

- **Slides change automatically or with a key.** In **Content → Ambient playlist → Change slides**, choose **Automatically** (every few seconds, now up to 5 minutes) or **With a key**: each press of the key you pick shows the next slide on every display, while no QR is showing. The key is only taken from Windows while this is on.
- **Text size per slide.** Rating, text and Your UPI QR slides have **Small**, **Medium** or **Large** words.
- **Slide messages wrap.** On E-ink displays, a message takes up to three lines, broken only between words, instead of being squeezed onto one.

## Fixes

- The Settings icon is an even gear again.
